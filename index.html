PERX Business Participation Agreement

This Business Participation Agreement (Agreement) is entered between PERX and the participating business identified during signup (Business). By completing this onboarding workflow, Business agrees to provide the selected offer to eligible PERX members and to participate according to the terms below.

1. Offer Fulfillment: Business will honor the offer submitted in this application for valid PERX member transactions, subject to normal inventory and operating limitations.

2. Accuracy of Information: Business certifies all submitted business information, ownership/authorization statements, and contact details are accurate and current.

3. Authorized Representative: Signer certifies under penalty of perjury that they are the business owner or an authorized representative with authority to enter legally binding agreements.

4. Verification and Review: PERX may perform identity, ownership, and business record verification, including domain checks, business listing checks, duplicate analysis, and fraud screening. Submissions may remain pending until manual review completes.

5. Activation: No business is activated in PERX until approved by a PERX administrator.

6. Audit Trail and Records: PERX retains signed agreement records, metadata, verification status, and submission logs for compliance, security, and operational purposes.

7. Electronic Signatures: Business agrees that an electronic signature and related electronic consent have the same legal effect as a handwritten signature.

8. Modifications: Agreement versions may change over time. Business remains bound to the version accepted at signature time unless superseded by a later signed agreement.

9. Contact and Support: PERX may contact Business regarding onboarding, verification, compliance, or partnership operations.

10. Termination: PERX reserves the right to reject or remove participation for suspected fraud, abuse, legal non-compliance, or policy violations.
