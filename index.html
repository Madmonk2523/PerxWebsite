WHAT IS PERX?

The future of rewards is nearby.

PERX is a location-powered rewards app that helps you discover nearby perks, offers, and experiences from businesses around you. As you move through your day, PERX surfaces rewards tied to real places in Long Island!

Whether it's your favorite coffee shop, a local restaurant, or a nearby Barbershop, PERX makes discovering and redeeming local rewards feel effortless.

No coupons. No punch cards. No searching. Just rewards built into everyday life.

How it works

Open the app, see what's active nearby, and unlock rewards from participating businesses in your area through everyday movement. PERX is designed to make local discovery simple, seamless, and rewarding.

Why people join

PERX helps people discover new local spots, save money at places they already visit or have never been before, and unlock rewards throughout their normal routine.

Built for Long Island

PERX starts locally with a focus on real neighborhoods, real businesses, and the places people actually go every day. Our goal is to create a more connected and rewarding local experience.
