How does a PERX grow?

Customers build up a reward as they pass participating businesses. When the reward becomes worth using, it gives them a reason to come in instead of passing by again.

FAQ

What does PERX cost?

Nothing during the pilot. There is currently no payment required to participate.

How does PERX work?

A customer's PERX can grow as they pass participating businesses. When they decide to visit, they can redeem their accumulated PERX on a qualifying purchase.

How much do I have to offer?

You approve the maximum PERX your business is willing to honor. PERX will never increase that maximum without your approval.

Is PERX just a coupon?

PERX is designed around local discovery and proximity. Instead of simply publishing the same discount to everyone, a customer's PERX builds through nearby activity before redemption.

Can I stop participating?

During the pilot, contact PERX if you'd like to pause your business or request a change to your offer.
